<objective>
Core-side hardening for large photos: lower the decode limits to 64 MP and 256 MiB and classify every limit violation (pixels, edge, allocation) as "too large", build previews with one copy instead of two, and shrink the remaining oversized render tests to at most 64 px.

Purpose: PERF-01 (memory bound with real camera photos, M3 pixel-buffer copy) and POLI-02 (M4 render-test sizes). D-08 and D-17 are reversible owner decisions (one constant each plus tests and docs; the docs entry follows in 06-07). D-18 makes the edge limit report "too large" so 06-02 can show the right German text.
Output: changed `decode.rs` (constants, explicit edge and buffer checks, tests), `images.rs` (`preview_buffer` + tests), `render.rs` (test sizes only).

Decisions implemented: D-08, D-17, D-18 (core classification); Claude's discretion M3 (pixel-buffer copy) and M4 (render-test sizes).
</objective>
