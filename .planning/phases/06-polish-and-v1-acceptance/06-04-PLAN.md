<objective>
Real phone photos open in the editor: Kotlin transcodes HEIC/WebP/AVIF picks and downscales anything above 64 MP while copying into the app cache, and failed copies are counted and reported instead of silently dropped.

Purpose: PERF-01 (large camera photos load, D-09) and ERRM-01 (transcode/copy failure is a visible, counted "unreadable" case, D-03). Without this, phone HEIC photos are skipped and 200 MP captures are rejected.
Output: transcoding/downscaling in `MainActivity.copyToCache`, JNI `nativeImagesPicked(paths, failed)`, `PlatformEvent::ImagesPicked { paths, failed }`, failed copies in the load notice.

Decisions implemented: D-01, D-02, D-03, D-04 (no libheif, no `webp` feature), D-09, D-16. D-01/D-09 put this transcoding into Kotlin by owner decision (it needs the ContentResolver); `docs/CODING_GUIDELINES.md` §5 is updated for it in 06-07.
</objective>
