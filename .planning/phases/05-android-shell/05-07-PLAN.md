  <instructions>
    Claude has already built and gate-checked the APK in 05-06. On each device (one with Android 14, one with Android 16):
    1. Enable Developer options (Settings > About phone > tap "Build number" 7 times) and turn on USB debugging (and "Wireless debugging" if you use Wi-Fi).
    2. Either USB: in an admin PowerShell on Windows run `usbipd list`, then `usbipd bind --busid <BUSID>` (once) and `usbipd attach --wsl --busid <BUSID>`.

    3. Accept the "Allow USB debugging?" RSA prompt on the device.
    4. Keep both devices unlocked with the screen on (screen timeout long enough) while Claude runs Task 2, so launch and back can be observed.
    5. Reply with "done" and, if you know them, which serial is the Android 14 and which the Android 16 device.
  </instructions>

<task type="checkpoint:human-verify" gate="blocking">
  <name>Task 3: Owner verifies touch editing, picker, save, share, back and edge-to-edge on both devices</name>
  <what-built>dimuli installed on your Android 14 and Android 16 devices (debug build from 05-06/05-07 Task 2). Picker, save to gallery, share, back handling, landscape layout and touch editing are implemented and passed on the API 33/36/37 emulators.</what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
    Do this on each device (Android 14 and Android 16) with your fingers; never use a mouse wheel or Bluetooth mouse scrolling (known Slint crash):
    1. Open dimuli from the launcher: label "dimuli" with the collage-grid icon; start screen text, button and Slint badge not under the status bar or camera cutout.
    2. Tap "Bilder wählen": the system Photo Picker opens with your photos; select 3-5 images and confirm: the editor opens with them. Repeat once and press back inside the picker: start screen unchanged, no message. (HEIC/WebP photos are skipped with a notice - expected, D-12.)
    3. Drag a divider handle: the cells resize smoothly and stop at the minimum size.
    4. Pinch with two fingers on an image: it zooms around the fingers; drag with one finger: the image pans and never shows an empty edge.
    5. Long-press an image (about half a second), drag it onto another cell and release: the two images swap.
    6. Long-press an image, and while still holding, put a second finger down: no crash and no swap highlight left behind; tell us what happened (swap, nothing, zoom).
    7. Tap a different template in the template bar; open "Einstellungen" (chevron flips) and change format, spacing, corners and background colour: the canvas updates live.
    8. Rotate the phone to landscape: the collage and all edits stay; canvas on the left, template bar and settings on the right; rotate back.
    9. Tap "Speichern": the busy overlay appears and taps on the canvas do nothing while it is visible; then "Collage gespeichert." appears, no permission dialog. Open Google Photos: the collage is in the "dimuli" album/folder (Pictures/dimuli).
    10. Tap "Teilen": the system share sheet opens with an image preview; close it: no "geteilt" message appears.
    11. Press back (gesture or button) in the editor: "Collage verwerfen?" appears; "Abbrechen" keeps the collage, "Verwerfen" returns to the start screen; back on the start screen closes the app.
